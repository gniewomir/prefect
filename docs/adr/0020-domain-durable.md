# Domain is a Stack Durable (zone + Stack-authored records)

Public DNS was out of band (operator pointed names at the Reserved IP outside the Stack). We make **Domain** a Stack-managed **Durable**: the provider DNS zone and the Stack-authored records under it. Park keeps Domains; Apply reattaches them; Teardown removes them; they stay on the Environment Cloud Project while Parked (no Host required). An Environment may have zero or more Domains. A records to the Environment’s Reserved IP are required for each declared name; a Domain may carry more Stack-authored records over time. Domains do not assign names to Workloads. Registrar purchase and NS delegation to the provider stay operator out of band. Apply does not auto-adopt existing provider zones (fail closed on collision); one-shot `terraform import` is documented Stack surgery. How an Environment declares which Domains it owns: [ADR-0021](0021-environment-domain-config.md). DigitalOcean first; the Durable *concept* stays provider-agnostic. ACME consumes the Domain assignment for its want-list ([ADR-0023](0023-acme-want-list-from-domains.md) / [#44](https://github.com/gniewomir/prefect/issues/44)); operator-owned reachability is [ADR-0022](0022-operator-owned-routes.md).

**Considered:** Zone-only Durable (leaves pointing tribal); records-only under an unmanaged zone; per-FQDN Durables (Workload-shaped name claims); Manifest-driven records; Apply auto-adopt of tribal zones (ADR-0018 dual path). Rejected.
